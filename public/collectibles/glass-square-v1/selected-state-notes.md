# Selected states

Built-in image editing: subtle yellow internal illumination, three separate variants referencing their matching default image. PNG originals and 96px WebP exports. Interactive preview only; not integrated into the live portfolio.

## Prompt

Use case: lighting-weather
Precise image edit: create the SELECTED / CLICKED state of this exact glass-box portfolio icon.
Only change illumination: a subtle warm butter-yellow light appears within the glass edges and the thin clear floor, with a very faint pale honey wash on the empty cream interior. Think an elegant glass collectible cabinet quietly lighting up when selected. Yellow should be visible at 32px but gentle, about 15% visual intensity. Keep the central backdrop near ivory. Keep the light localized to the box, especially the inner rim and floor; no large outer halo. Preserve transparent-looking glass reflections.
CRITICAL invariants: identical square case geometry, position, scale, frame, viewpoint, image margins and square canvas. Identical character, pose, face, clothes, gear, all details and their original colors. Do not redraw or redesign any shapes. The original and selected states must align when swapped. Keep outside background neutral #F5F5F4; do not tint the whole image. No added outline, badge, text, checkmark, rays, glitter, black plinth, nameplate or new object. Warm understated yellow illumination only. Output one edited icon.
