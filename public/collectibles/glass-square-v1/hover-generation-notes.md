# Hover artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, one edit per activity using its original PNG as the reference. Files: photography-hover-v1.png, cycling-hover-v1.png, backpacking-hover-v1.png.

The returned images contain an opaque checkerboard instead of alpha. The website clips each image to its glass silhouette in CSS; do not use the raw masters as transparent assets.

## Prompt

The same prompt was used for all three, substituting photography, cycling, or backpacking:

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: supplied photography glass collectible icon. Create ONE production hover-state square icon. Preserve the exact character, clothing, pose, equipment, restrained illustrated rendering, neutral warm colors and glass material of the original. Rotate the entire glass case AND contained figurine together about its vertical axis by only 12 degrees, revealing a little more of the right side face; slight 4 degree view from above. This must feel like the same object gently turned, not a redesigned object. Keep vertical edges upright, keep same overall apparent size and centered composition, case occupies about 84% of square canvas. Front face remains dominant, full figure fully visible. Keep delicate glass edges and subtle reflections. No black base, no labels, no lettering, no sparkles, no scenery, no new objects, no heavy cast shadow, no yellow glow. Exterior outside the glass case must be genuinely transparent alpha, not white, not checkerboard. Interior pale ivory remains. No opaque white strip outside or below case. Standalone asset, not a sheet or website mockup.
