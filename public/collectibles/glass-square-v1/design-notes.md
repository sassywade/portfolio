## Photography

Use case: stylized-concept
Design ONE refined square collectible icon of a tiny illustrated photographer INSIDE A REAL, VISIBLE GLASS DISPLAY BOX. Reference 1 is the character to preserve; reference 2 is the collectible-in-glass idea to refine. This is a personal portfolio artifact inspired by childhood die-cast model cases.
Essential design: the actual GLASS CASE must have a SQUARE front face (equal width and height), soft small corner radii, shallow visible depth along the right and top sides, thin translucent warm silver edges, a single understated white reflection to make the glass readable. Nearly straight-on view, only a little depth. It should feel like an exquisite small glass object, not a generic app tile. The whole box fills 85% of a square image.
Inside the glass box, a fully visible miniature of the curly-haired warm brown-skinned man from reference 1 in cream overshirt, faded light-blue jeans, white sneakers, compact black camera at chest, in a relaxed three-quarter standing pose. Recognizable character and clothing, simplified painterly editorial illustration with coherent color masses and restrained facial features. Preserve warmth and personality; don't turn him into a stick figure, geometric person or public-sign pictogram. Full body roughly 72% of the box's height, well centered.
Base: a very THIN frosted clear-glass floor / pale stone sliver, only 3% of the case height, continuous with glass case. NO black plinth, NO nameplate, NO text. No dark heavy perimeter. Interior empty warm off-white; no SF skyline, no bridge, no landscape.
Overall craft: sophisticated quiet materiality, subtle reflections on transparent glass, warm painterly little human in the middle; minimal and clean with enough detail to feel personal at 36px. Low contrast container, character provides color. Matte illustration, not plastic toy/chibi, not emoji, not shiny 3D render. Isolated on solid portfolio background #F5F5F4 with almost no cast shadow. No checkerboard, no labels, no brand logo. ONE icon only.
